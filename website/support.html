Frequently Asked Questions

🎤 Why is the app not recognizing my voice in speaking exercises?
To practice speaking, iOS requires two specific permissions:
  • Microphone Permission: Allows audio input.
  • Speech Recognition Permission: Converts spoken phonemes into text.
To check your permissions: open your iPhone Settings > Odi and ensure both Microphone and Speech Recognition toggles are turned on (Green).
🔊 I cannot hear any sound from the pronunciation buttons.
Please check the following:
  • Ensure your iPhone volume is turned up using the physical volume buttons on the side of your device.
  • Make sure your phone is not connected to a silent Bluetooth device or AirPods in your bag.
  • Odi is optimized to play through the main loudspeaker even in silent mode, but if you still hear no sound, try restarting the app.
🧠 How does the Spaced Repetition System (SRS) work?
Odi uses a spaced repetition algorithm to schedule word reviews at scientifically calculated intervals. When you practice a word correctly, its review interval expands (1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 16 days, etc.). If you struggle with a word, the app brings it back for review sooner so it moves into your permanent memory.
✈️ Can I practice Serbian offline?
Yes! The first 5 foundational courses and their complete audio sets are bundled directly into the app. You can also download the remaining 35 courses while connected to Wi-Fi to enjoy completely offline learning on planes or road trips.
🔄 How do I reset my learning progress?
If you want to start fresh from Unit 1, go to the Settings tab inside Odi and select Reset Learning Progress. This will re-initialize your spaced repetition intervals and word mastery statistics.
